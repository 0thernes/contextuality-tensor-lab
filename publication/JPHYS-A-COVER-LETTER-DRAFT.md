Please consider "Exact contextuality degrees for disjoint Mermin configurations"
for Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical.

The manuscript gives exact arbitrary-copy contextuality degrees for full families
of union contexts formed from disjoint Peres–Mermin squares and Mermin pentagrams.
It addresses the limit question stated in Section 6 of Muller and Saniga,
arXiv:2609.19862v1, using a simple augmented-score product identity. The square and
pentagram degree formulas are (7^k−5^k)/2 and (6^k−4^k)/2. A general argument covers
the limiting normalized gap for any finite contextual seed. The paper also shows
why this limit does not improve independent elementary readout-error thresholds.

The supporting repository contains Lean 4 proofs with a complete project-theorem
axiom audit, exact finite certificates, independent matrix and full-support checks,
and an operational four-qubit simulation using the Tsotchke stack. The manuscript
states exactly which propositions are formalized. The result is proposed as an
exact solution of the specified construction, without an unqualified priority claim.
